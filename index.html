Napoléon Bonaparte

Emperor of the French
Born 15 August 1769
Died 5 May 1821
Reign 1804 – 1814/1815
Titles Emperor, General
“Impossible is a word to be found only in the dictionary of fools.”
Napoleon Bonaparte was a French military commander and political leader who rose to prominence during the French Revolution and led several successful campaigns. He was Emperor of the French from 1804 to 1814, and again briefly in 1815. His legal reform, the Napoleonic Code, influenced civil law systems worldwide. He is remembered as one of the greatest military tacticians in history.
